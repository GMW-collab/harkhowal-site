Dera Sant Ghar, Harkhowal

Sant Jawala Singh Ji left the army after serving in France during the First World War and joined the Dera at Hoti Mardan under Sant Aiya Singh Ji. In 1918 he settled between the villages of Harkhowal and Pandori Bibi, about 11 km southwest of Hoshiarpur.

Dera Sant Ghar grew as sangat came for his simple, clear discourses, and thousands took Amrit at his hands. Today Sant Baba Jagjit Singh Ji continues this seva through katha of Gurbani and Gurmat vichar.
